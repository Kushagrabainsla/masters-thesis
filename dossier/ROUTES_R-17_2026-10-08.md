## 1. Method

1. Frontier map. I searched the 2025 and 2026 programs of OSDI, NSDI, SOSP, EuroSys, ASPLOS and MLSys
   and the newest preprints. I looked for new environments (new models, new hardware, new workloads)
   and for limits that the newest papers state.
2. First-principles generation. For each environment I asked what physical or workload property
   changed, and which proven mechanism from another field fits it.
3. Round 1: six candidates (S1 to S6). A Sonnet reviewer attacked them with its own searches.
   Write-up: `dossier/REVIEW_R-17_SHORTLIST_2026-10-08.md`.
4. Round 2: the main session probed five more directions after the round 1 kills.
5. Round 3: a second Sonnet agent (idea generation is a use beyond the attack role; the main session
   then checked its survivors) generated and self-screened candidates in the areas that rounds 1
   and 2 did not cover (training, collectives, numerics, agent infrastructure outside the inference
   server, GPU cluster operations). Write-up: `dossier/R-17_ROUND3_SONNET_CANDIDATES_2026-10-08.md`.
6. Spot checks. The main session opened the two sources that the round 1 kill of S2 and S6 rests on
   (GVR 2604.22312 and 2609.18849) and confirmed them.

## 6. Best survivor and its premise check

The best survivor is I-83 (rank-safe block-max pruning for the DSA lightning indexer). It is WEAK and
needs a GPU. No candidate that can be checked on the Mac survived.

### 6.1 Exact gap
On real DeepSeek-V3.2 (or V4) indexer keys, can a sign-correct, rank-safe block bound skip enough key
blocks to beat GVR end to end at 128K to 1M context, with the same top-k output?

What prior work answers: GVR gives exact top-k faster but still scores every key. HISA and LongCat SA
skip blocks but are not rank-safe. IndexCache skips layers. Quest uses page bounds to rank, not to prune
safely. What is not answered: whether exact skipping of key blocks is possible at all with real keys.

### 6.2 Why the Mac cannot decide it
- FACT (reviewer): DeepSeek-V3.2 is about 690 GB in FP8. No small open DSA model was found. No public
  dump of indexer q, w and k tensors was found (GitHub search by the main session, 2026-10-08).
- A layer-0 check is possible with real weights (layer-0 inputs are token embeddings, so no full forward
  pass is needed), but it needs a download of the shard with the embeddings and layer 0 (ask the user
  first). It is asymmetric: layer-0 keys depend only on token identity and position, so a FAIL there
  says little about deeper layers, and only a PASS is informative. I do not recommend it as a kill test.
- INTERPRETATION: there is a structural reason to expect failure. The indexer key goes through
  LayerNorm, RoPE on 64 of 128 dims and a Hadamard rotation. Inside a block of 16 consecutive tokens,
  the high-frequency RoPE dims rotate a lot, so box and ball bounds stay loose. The reviewer's synthetic
  test found bounds 26 to 57 times the k-th score.

### 6.3 Proposed premise check (not run, not yet predeclared in Section 6.1 of the journal)
Run when GPU time exists (first grant hours, or a short rental of one 8-GPU H200 node for about 3 hours).
1. Dump the indexer q, w and k tensors for 4 layers (early, middle, late, last) on 20 long prompts
   (code, chat, long documents, needle tasks) at 32K, 128K, 512K and 1M tokens where the memory allows.
2. Do the bound analysis offline on the Mac with numpy: box bound with a sign split, ball bound with a
   sign split, and k-means cluster bounds; block sizes 8, 16, 32, 64; K = 512 and 2048; cold start
   versus warm start from the previous decode step (as GVR does).
3. Kill rules (heuristics from R-17, to be frozen in the journal before any run):
   - KILL if the sign-correct bound skips fewer than 50% of blocks at B = 16, L = 128K, K = 2048.
   - KILL if the net key-byte saving (skipped bytes minus bound metadata) is under 25% at L = 128K.
   - KILL if the cold-start skip rate is under 20% of the warm-start rate at L = 128K.
4. Edges to check before accepting "no effect": all four context lengths (the skip rate should rise
   with L because K is fixed), all block sizes and bound types, per-layer distributions (not one mean),
   prefill and decode separately, and at least 20 prompts.
5. If it passes: build a kernel and compare end to end against GVR, not only against radix-select.

## 7. Lesson

Three route searches (R-10, R-13, R-17) found almost nothing. In 2026, a direct transfer of a
well-known mechanism into LLM serving or agent infrastructure appears as a preprint within weeks.
The only open candidates are second-order (an exactness guarantee, as in I-83) and need hardware
that the Mac cannot simulate.
