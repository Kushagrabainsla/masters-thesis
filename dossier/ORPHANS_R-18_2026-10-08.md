method: researchers such as Karpathy find problems by running systems. As a cheaper proxy, read the

## 2. What the issue scan found (main session)

- Sorting by reactions returns roadmaps, model-support requests and installation bugs. The
  performance-labelled issues are mostly regressions and kernel tuning. They are engineering, not
  research gaps. INTERPRETATION.
- **Hybrid-model full re-prefill** (llama.cpp #22746, 126 comments; #21831; vLLM #38988, #37121).
  FACT: users measured a 31.8% cache-miss rate and 20.5 s of wasted prefill per turn on Qwen3.6 with an
  agent harness; a community fix cut the miss rate to 1.9%. The root cause is an implementation defect
  in recurrent-state snapshots (a position shift), with PR #26499 in progress. The research part (where
  to place recurrent-state snapshots) is I-15, killed on 2026-09-17 (simple policies win; Sparse Prefix
  Caching does exact placement). Not new. Registered as I-95, KILL.
- **Silent numeric bugs in Megatron parallelism composition** (Megatron-LM #7452, 2026-09-17). FACT:
  13 silent bugs (wrong loss, gradients or weights, no error), found with Meta's `spmd_types` type
  checker plus an AI fuzzer, each with a CPU reproduction. Context for I-21 (reconfiguration
  equivalence) and I-23. Meta (ezyang) works on it directly, so the scoop risk is high. Registered as
  I-96, KILL as a thesis route, kept as context.
- Other leads were small engineering fixes or already killed: NCCL out of memory while PyTorch holds
  cached memory (TorchTitan #5019, AMem in Megatron #2423), duplicate cold-prefix loads (vLLM #50433,
  same as I-64), broadcast checkpoint load (PyTorch #171412, round 3 C03), checkpoint-aware preemption
  (I-93).

## 5. Lesson

- Issue trackers show bugs and regressions. They do not show research gaps.
- Characterization papers sit close to their fixes. Three of the five headline numbers checked by the
  reviewer were misread by the harvest. Read the primary text before trusting a number.
- Use these sources to size a waste after an idea exists, not to find the idea.
- Four searches (R-10, R-13, R-17, R-18) found no open gap that the Mac can test.
