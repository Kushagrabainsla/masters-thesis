  environment, change it a little if necessary, and show a visible gain. Do not make a benchmarking
  paper the contribution (D-29). Combinations of existing ideas are allowed (D-32).

  1. Rubber duck before the run. Write the pass and kill rules into the journal (Section 6.1) before
     any run. Freeze the decision script and record its checksum.

     `model: sonnet`). Fix the findings and log them as amendments. Do a second pass after big fixes.
  3. Run. Then rubber duck the results step by step.

  grid (trigger size, horizon, metric power) before you explain the absence (Section 8.1 row 46).
- Never delete journal history. Corrections go to Section 8.1. An AI recommendation is not a decision;
  log who decided in Section 10.
- Use absolute paths in shell commands. Do not use `cd` in compound commands (user request).
