# Advisor support letter (DRAFT for the advisor to edit and sign)

This is a starting draft that the student prepared. The advisor must read it, change anything that is not accurate, and sign it on institutional letterhead as a PDF. It should not be sent unedited on the advisor's behalf.

One letter can serve both the NSF ACCESS Explore request and the NAIRR Pilot Start-Up request. ACCESS suggests this wording: "I am aware of (student's name)'s interest in utilizing ACCESS resources in pursuit of their dissertation activities and intend to engage with and guide their computational and research activity." The version below adapts it to a master's thesis.

---

[Institutional letterhead: San Jose State University, Department of Computer Science]

[Date]

To the ACCESS Allocations Committee and the NAIRR Pilot Review Team:

I am writing in support of the allocation requests submitted by Kushagra Bainsla, a graduate student in the M.S. in Computer Science program at San Jose State University, for the project "Certifying Output Equivalence of Fast LLM Inference Kernels on Hopper and Blackwell GPUs."

I am aware of Kushagra's interest in utilizing ACCESS and NAIRR Pilot resources in pursuit of their master's thesis activities, and I intend to engage with and guide their computational and research activity. I will meet with them [weekly / every two weeks], review the experimental design and results, and be responsible for ensuring the resources are used for the stated research and in line with the program's policies.

The project asks whether swapping one GPU kernel or backend for another inside an LLM inference server changes model outputs, and how much speed a correctness-aware selection can recover. It requires Hopper and Blackwell GPUs, which are not available to our group locally. The student has already built and validated the evaluation harness on a laptop, defined the decision criteria before running experiments, and scoped the request to a short, decisive first study.
